Pet Care Guide & Checklist

Essential guidelines for bringing home a new companion.

Daily Routines & Nutrition

  • Feed balanced age-appropriate food twice daily.
  • Provide fresh, clean drinking water at all times.
  • Schedule at least 30-60 minutes of daily exercise or outdoor walks.

Litter Box & Environment

  • Scoop litter box daily; full litter refresh weekly.
  • Provide scratching posts to support natural clawing instincts.
  • Offer elevated perches or quiet spaces for rest.
